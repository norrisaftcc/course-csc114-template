- **Prompt Masters:** Use GitHub Desktop, the web interface drag-and-drop, or the "Add file" button. All of these are fine. See `for_prompt_masters_week_01.md` for click-by-click steps.

## What we're looking for when we read it

We're not grading whether your agent is "good." We're looking at four things across all the files:

**1. AI partnership quality.** Does your testing log show that you tested your agent on purpose, not just hoped it worked? Did you try the trick question?

**2. Problem-solving process.** Can we see the one-change rule in your three system prompt versions? Can we point at v1 → v2 and name what changed and why?

**3. Professional communication.** Is the README understandable to someone who doesn't know your domain? Is the reflection specific to *your* week, not generic?

**4. Critical thinking.** Did you turn off the tools your agent didn't need (and say why)? Did you choose constraints that actually fit your domain, or did you copy generic ones?

These are the same four categories we'll use all semester. Don't try to hit them — just do the work and they'll come out naturally.

## What the reflection should contain

Four to six sentences, no more. Address at least two of these prompts (you don't have to do all):

- What's one thing that surprised you about your agent's behavior?
- Which change between versions made the biggest difference? Why do you think that is?
- If you had another week, what would your v4 look like?
- What's a question you have now that you didn't have at the start of the week?
- Is there a real-world problem you'd build an agent for? What and why?

A reflection that says "this week was hard but I learned a lot" doesn't count. A reflection that says "I was surprised that adding 'never make up sources' to my constraints stopped the agent from inventing URLs but didn't stop it from inventing course names — I think I need a separate constraint for that" counts.

## What gets a re-do, what gets a check

Week 1 is **complete/re-do**, not letter-graded. There are two outcomes:

- **Complete.** All 9 files are present, the iterations look real, the reflection is specific. You move on to Week 2.
- **Re-do.** Something's missing or the iterations look like they were done all at once. You get notes from the instructor and a window to fix it. No penalty.

The instructor will reach out in office hours or on GitHub Discussions if you're heading toward a re-do. Don't panic if it happens — it's normal, especially in Week 1. Sacred Flow makes this whole loop easier starting Week 2.
