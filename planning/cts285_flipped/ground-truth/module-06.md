# 06 — Sprint 3 Close — The Live Demo

**Loop 1 Close · Sprint 3 Review · Weeks 11–12 · 125 pts.** Role: Team Lead.
Presentation, glows/grows, and the full Loop 1 retrospective.

## ⬡ Before Class — Async

> **NTS Floor 5.** The retrospective room. Every student presents Data Man to their
> review group. Peers give glows/grows. Loop 1 closes.
> *"Your demo crashes halfway through. What do you do?"*

- ▶ Glows/Grows Practice — given transcript, identify 2 specific glows + 2 actionable grows · *MLO 6.3 · Sim*
- ▶ Retrospective Action Item Quality Check — classify 6 items, select improved versions · *MLO 6.4 · Sim*
- ▶ Q&A Response — Technical Question — "flat file vs. database?" sim · *MLO 6.2 · Sim*
- S Presentation Self-Assessment Practice — apply glows/grows rubric to sample video first · *MLO 6.1/6.3 · Canvas · completion graded*

**👥 Review Group Format:** Groups of 3–4. Instructor spot-checks one presentation per
group live. Reads all peer forms per student. Adds synthesis comment connecting peer
observations to CLOs. Peer forms due: 48 hours after presentation. Instructor synthesis
due: 72 hours after peer forms.

## ⬡ In Class — Facilitated

- 🎤 **Sprint 3 Presentations — Review Groups** — 5-7 min live demo per student.
  Hybrid: in-person primary, recorded for remote. Instructor spot-checks one per group.
  Full glows/grows rubric.
- 🗣 **Full Retrospective — Loop 1 Arc** — Class-wide retrospective covers Modules 1-6.
  What changed sprint over sprint? Lucidchart board.

**⚡ RSI Touchpoint:** Instructor reads all peer forms per student and adds written
synthesis comment connecting observations to CLOs. For students not directly observed,
this is the primary RSI touchpoint. *Response window: 72 hrs · Platform: Canvas Peer Review Comment.*

### MLOs

- **6.1** Demo system live; explain decisions; show acceptance criteria · *Apply · CLO 5*
- **6.3** Apply glows/grows framework with specific evidence · *Evaluate · CLO 5*
- **6.4** Conduct full sprint retrospective with action items · *Evaluate · CLO 1*
- **6.5** Reflect Sprint 1 vs. Sprint 3 growth with artifact evidence · *Evaluate · CLO 5*

## ⬡ After Class — Canvas Submissions

- **Sprint 3 Presentation — Data Man Live Demo** — 5-7 min; graded by instructor with glows/grows rubric · *50 pts · Presentation*
- **Peer Glows/Grows Forms** — one form per review group member; graded on specificity and evidence; due 48 hrs after presentation · *25 pts · Peer Review · Gates retrospective*
- **Full Sprint Retrospective — M1–6 Arc** — Lucidchart board — what went well, didn't, will change; 3+ items per section with owners + success criteria · *30 pts · Rubric · CLO 1*
- **Personal Growth Reflection** — 300-word Sprint 1 vs. Sprint 3 comparison citing ≥3 course artifacts as evidence · *20 pts · Rubric · CLO 5*

## Thread & gates

- **Data Man Thread:** Loop 1 closes. Data Man demonstrated live in review groups. Full
  retrospective covers M1-6 arc. Students leave ready for Loop 2. ↳ *Deliverable:
  Presentation + peer forms + retrospective + reflection.*
- **Unlocks when:** Outline submitted + instructor commented. **Peer forms gate
  retrospective.** **Gates M7 on:** Full retrospective submitted.
